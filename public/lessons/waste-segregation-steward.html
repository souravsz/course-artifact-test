Your role

The steward's playbook

Rules don't change habits. People do. These four moves are how good stewards bring a whole building along. Open each one.

0 / 4 opened

  1. People listen to someone they know. Learn residents' names, be at the gate on time, and smile before you say anything about the bag. Trust comes before correction.

  2. A two-minute demo at their own kitchen bin beats any rulebook. Point at real items: "This peel goes here, this cover goes there." Make it look easy, because it is.

  3. Thank them for what's right before you mention what's wrong, and never correct someone in front of their neighbours. Embarrassed people stop trying.

  4. Note which homes are segregating and which need a follow-up visit. This data shows where the community needs support. It's never for naming and shaming.
